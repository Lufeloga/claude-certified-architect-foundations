## Reading the result

Study the weakest domain, not the lowest total. A domain below 60% is where the
next hour of reading belongs; the [Frame Map](../frame-map/) has a frame for
each.

Do not convert this to a scaled score. The certification's scale is its own, and
a number that looks official invites a conclusion this set cannot support in
either direction.
